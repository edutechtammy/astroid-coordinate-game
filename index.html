What position is the asteroid in?

Game Instructions & Keyboard Navigation

Game Instructions:

  • An asteroid will appear at a random position on the coordinate grid.
  • Enter the X and Y coordinates of the asteroid in the input boxes.
  • Press Submit to check your answer.
  • If correct, the spaceship will move and fire at the asteroid, destroying it.
  • If incorrect, try again until you get both coordinates right.
  • You can toggle sound on or off using the checkbox.

Keyboard Controls:

  • Use Tab to move between input fields and buttons.
  • Enter your X and Y answers using the keyboard.
  • Press Enter or Space on the Submit button to check your answer.
  • Press Escape to close this instructions window.
  • Use the Sound On checkbox to toggle game sounds.
